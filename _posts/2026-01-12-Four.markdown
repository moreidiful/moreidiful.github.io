Today I needed quiet moments and I took them. I think sometimes the world becomes overwhelming but we feel the need to push through anyways. Maybe because we're afraid of missing out, or because we think it's best for us. But I've been trying to allow myself small moments of peace day by day. I'm trying to stick by the importance of taking care of myself. Of cherishing what I do have. I don't always succeed, but I'm trying.

At the end of last year I decided to make a commitment to treating myself better during 2026. And I suppose it's not a huge feat, but it's something I've never done before. I've mae concious efforts to improve, but the scale has never been so large. And sometimes when I think about it it feels daunting. For this long? For this many days? But then I have a quiet moment and I feel alright again.

Instead of looking at it as a streak I'm going to do it like it's an exam. Points out of 365. The number of days lost doesn't feel so big, then. One out of many, rather than a big failure that ruins a streak of better days. I think getting an 80% would be something to be proud of. And getting something lower doens't mean I failed. It's just a smaller percentage, and something I can improve upon next year. These things are all about learning and failing. So I won't worry too much about it. I'll listen to good music instead, and I'll let myself relax when it gets harder.

I think, if I stick by the people who care about me, and I do not let my creative spirit die then it will be a good year. There is no other outcome! It will be so!
